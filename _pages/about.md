I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Massachusetts Lowell, USA. Prior to joining UMass Lowell in September 2026, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Epidemiology at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health, where I worked with Dr. Debashree Ray.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Connecticut in August 2023 (advisor: Dr. Dipak K. Dey; co-advisor: Dr. Victor Hugo Lachos Dávila). I completed my M.S. in Statistics at Kyungpook National University, South Korea, in 2017 (advisor: Dr. Dalho Kim), and my B.S. in Statistics at the same institution in 2015.

My research focuses on developing statistical methodologies for complex biomedical and genetic data. In statistical genetics, I am particularly interested in identifying shared genetic associations across multiple traits using GWAS summary statistics, with an emphasis on composite null hypothesis testing, correlated data, and complex study designs. My broader methodological interests include finite mixture models, survival analysis, high-dimensional regression, and variable selection.
